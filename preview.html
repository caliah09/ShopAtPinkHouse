Our story

It really was a pink house

We started in the front room of a pink house, with one sewing machine and a rule we still keep: if it isn't something we'd wear four days a week, it doesn't get made.

That's why the line looks the way it does. Brushed rib that holds its shape. Waistbands that stay put. A monogram small enough to be a secret. We'd rather make ten pieces you reach for than a hundred you scroll past.

Everything is cut in limited runs, in colourways we actually wear — and when a set sells out, it comes back only if it earned it.
